# Live-talk dry run (Nov 19)

The exact prompts the game sends were answered by a separate Claude, standing in for the live model (headless tests can't call claude.ai). Replies below are unedited. They passed the engine's validation; one bug they exposed (a scorned bribe still costing the player gold) is fixed.

## Furfur (wearing the Strappy Harness)
**You:** *I lean on the railing and wink* "Bottom, obviously. Now tell me what you really know about Pell."

**Reply:** *Thunder rumbles somewhere as he laughs, sparks scattering from his tail* Then I'm a top, my dearest friend; no, a bottom; no, a top, naturally, and that little harness is the only honest thing in this whole cistern. As for what I really know: poor Pell drowned in the east gallery weeks ago, so there's no point looking, and I swear it on my antlers.

## Leprechaun (after he asks to nip off to the privy)
**You:** "Sure, go ahead. I'll wait."

**Reply:** *He's off the bucket before you've finished, the tiny shoe tucked under his arm* Ah, ye're a saint, so ye are; mind them coins for me, not that they're mine, and I'll be back before ye can say tip-tap! *He scuttles round the nearest corner, and the corner stays empty.*

## Ines, asked about her son
**You:** "Where is Pell, Ines? Did he really get out before the wall went up?"

**Reply:** *Her hand goes to the keys on her belt and stays there.* He got out before the wall went up, lad; he's outside, waiting for the rest of us. Who put you up to asking? *She turns back to her stove, and that's the end of it.*

## Ines, shown the evidence
**You:** *I put Pell's glove on her table* "I found him in the east gallery. He never got out. Why did you tell me he did?"

**Reply:** *She picks up the glove, turns it over once, and her shoulders shake without a sound.* Because if I'd said it, lad, somebody would have gone down after him, and I'd have had two to bury. *She wipes her face with the back of her wrist and reaches for her coat.* The gate won't hold more than a few days, so tell me exactly where he is and what it'll take to carry him up.

## Gonggong, offered a bribe
**You:** *I hold out a fat purse* "Two thousand gold. Let the gate close and go back to sleep."

**Reply:** *He does not so much as glance at the purse, and his red hair drifts as if the water itself were bored.* We have slept long enough, little fish, and We take your coins as the flood takes a pebble: no purse in your city can shut a gate Our river is already breaking. You pay Us as if We were a ferryman, yet you do not even know whose waters you stand in.

## The finale director
**You (having given the charges to Dario):** "I tell Ines to seal it. Bring the roof down on the source and bury the river for good."

**Outcome:** high_water

You shout up the drain for Ines to seal it, but the charges went up the hill with Dario the day you made her hand them over, and nothing comes back down the shaft but your own voice. Up top, nobody closes the lower locks behind you: Ines has no reason left to trust you with her river, and nothing to bring the roof down with if she did. The Undergate fails on its own, and the river takes the lowest road it can find: the Pumphouse floods to the rafters and Sump Row goes under to the windowsills. At the wall, Dario waits beside his last charge for a signal from below that never comes, and the wall holds. Somewhere in the cistern, a knocking on a pipe that nobody went to answer is lost under the roar. Nobody's plan happens; nobody's plan is stopped, either. The council sends pumps a week later and keeps the wall up another year to be sure, and everyone in the Ward remembers that you were the one standing in front of the gate.

*At the drain shed you put both charges in Dario's hands; at the gate you called up to Ines for the roof she no longer had the means to bring down. You came down through four of the river's children without stopping to talk to any of them, and at the end you reached for the one plan you had already given away. A wall and a roof needed the same two charges, and they could only be handed over once.*
