# Playtest 2: The Flood Ward (AI playtester, headless, scripted mock brain)

Played Nov 19, 20:47–21:35 UTC (47 min wall clock) from New Game to the ending **"The Breach"**. I played as David
(docs/DAVID.md) on a different route from playtest 1:
- **Choices:** backed Dario in the drain-shed standoff, declined Hester's stone, sent Pell home to knock on Sump Row's doors, took Teo's wrench, exposed Teo to the Ward, and typed the finale in my own words.
- **Flirting:** Dario (scripted "Finish the line." and free talk), the Bake-danuki, the Leprechaun, Brokkr, the Troll, Ares, Furfur ("top or bottom?") and Gonggong ("strong and virile", David's own example).
- **Demons:** recruited 5 (Bake-danuki, Kappa, Gargoyle, Furfur, Kodama) and fused Kappa + Gargoyle into Hephaestus.
- **Harness:** wore the Strappy Harness through the Ward and the Culverts, then again for the Furfur and Gonggong talks.

The ending recorded: spoke with 19, recruited 5, killed 18, ran from 1 fight, let 4 walk away, caught 3 lies, Lv 9.

Caveat: free-form demon and NPC replies came from the keyword mock. I don't judge their wording, only the rules and
presentation around them. Where a problem is the mock's alone, I say so.

## Verdict (5 lines)
1. **Closer to "yes".** The story layer now delivers. Backing Dario changed four later scenes, Ines's soup note reached me underground, Gonggong's "what have you carried?" menu used my wrench and my Sump Row truth, and the Breach ending, fates and reflection matched my route and my typed words.
2. **The middle no longer drags.** A fight now comes about every 25 steps, no species appeared more than twice per floor, enemy weaknesses are remembered, and the Journal's "Unfinished" list made the trip back to the Ward feel deliberate instead of invisible.
3. **Dying is now the biggest blocker.** I had 3 game overs in 47 minutes. Two came while wearing the Strappy Harness: a Lv 8 Oni in the Cistern at my Lv 6, and a Lv 4 Brokkr in the Culverts. The third was a failed escape from a Lv 11 Minotaur at my Lv 8. Each death ended the game while 3 demons were still standing, and each cost 5–7 minutes of replay.
4. **Talk presentation still undercuts the free-form promise.** Talk openers contradict the scene that just played (the kettle, Furfur, the Banshee, Gonggong). Two of my three caught lies were caught *for* me by scripted scenes, and the Journal never records who claimed what.
5. **Fix deaths and the harness, opener continuity, and Journal claims, and David very likely says "Yes, I want more."** Today he would enjoy the story half and rage-quit at the second or third game over.

## Combat and fusion notes
- **Press-turn sweeps feel great now that affinities stick:**
  - Kettle Gust vs the Wind-weak Will-o'-the-Wisp (pass, Gust, pass, Gust, dead).
  - Furfur's Spark and Hephaestus's Thunder Edge vs the Gargoyle.
  - Gust plus Furfur's Ember vs Anubis: Fire- and Wind-weak, dead in one round.
- **Enemy weakness turns are brutal, and the protagonist is the single point of failure:**
  - A Lv 4 Kappa's Riverbank Sumo hit the kettle for 52 + 52.
  - The Oni's Twin Slash on harness-me: 55 + 82 crit, dead.
  - A Lv 4 Brokkr: Ember 33 + Bash crit 67, dead.
  - The Minotaur after a failed escape: Twin Slash 80 + 81 killed Furfur (the weakness gave it an extra action), then Cleave did 135 to me, dead.
  - That is SMT-faithful, but SMT gives you Recarm, revive items and Pixie early. Here, Puck is the only healer, I lost him to a reload, and I never saw him again.
- **Strappy Harness.**
  - Talk effects: start mood +1 for "into it" demons. It shows as a smiling face for Furfur and Gonggong, and the flirt bonus applies only to pure flirts. In mock mode no line ever mentions the outfit. Live Claude gets the `wearing` text, so verify there.
  - Cost: 3 weaknesses. It caused 2 deaths within about 15 minutes.
  - Because armor now swaps anywhere, the sensible play is to wear it only right before a known talk. That trivializes the trade-off, except that random encounters catch you in it.
  - Suggestion: one weakness (Phys), say plainly in the shop what "weak" means, and maybe allow swapping only at save points.
- **Fusion.**
  - Kappa (Lv 3) + Gargoyle → Hephaestus Lv 8, inheriting Mend, Thunder Edge and Hail. It's great, with a superb portrait, and Fusion now shows "needs Lv X" for results above your level.
  - At Lv 7 the only fusion I could make was Kappa + Furfur → Charon, which costs you your best demon. Fusion options are thin before Lv 8.
  - The Compendium's "Summon" prices are a good gold sink.
- **Growth.** Lv 6 → 9 over about 25 fights. The protagonist never learns a skill, so late Light-weak enemies (Minotaur, Troll) have no answer unless you happen to own a Valkyrie.
- **The Golem fight is a slog:** it resists Phys and Elec, and Hail does 22 per hit, five times.

## Prioritized top 10 (what stands between this and "Yes, I want more")
1. **Make death recoverable and the spikes fair.**
   - The game continues while any demon stands, plus a revive item or skill.
   - The harness has 1 weakness and the shop says what weak means.
   - Random encounters capped at player level +1: the Oni Lv 8 in the Cistern at Lv 6, and Minotaur/Anubis Lv 11 at Lv 8, are the killers.
   - Autosave on every stair.
   - Three game overs in 47 minutes is the single biggest reason David would quit.
2. **Opener continuity.** When a scene just played, the talk opener must continue it (the kettle, Furfur's "top or bottom?", the Banshee, Gonggong after the item menu), and never be empty. Pass the scene text or `talkNote` to both brains.
3. **Lies the player catches.**
   - Record NPC *claims* in the Journal with attribution ("Ines: Pell got out before the wall"), and mark them CAUGHT.
   - Let the soup note and the wrench scene *reveal* the truth, but leave the "you lied" moment to the player, with a payoff when he says it.
   - Make missing a lie cost something.
4. **Level gate.**
   - No demands from demons you can't command (or a refund).
   - Show the gate on the talk screen too.
   - Never consume a one-of-a-kind demon (Furfur) when the gate blocks him; let him wait or come back.
5. **Recruiting in hostile talks.**
   - Roll the join chance on the mood *after* the current message.
   - Apply the harness flirt bonus whenever the message includes a flirt.
   - Make the mock offer a demand or a join rather than "lets you pass" on the first friendly line, so headless tests and offline play can recruit normally.
6. **Protagonist growth.** Two or three learnable skills by Lv 9 (one Light), or a skill pick on level-up. Show "X learned Y" after battles.
7. **Item and Journal hygiene.**
   - Remove used keys.
   - Make key items unsellable.
   - Take the wrench away when you give it to Teo, or let the scene say you kept it.
   - Turn "What you've done" into readable decisions ("You backed Dario"), not button labels.
8. **Switches and leftover beats.**
   - Bespoke text for Teo's DON'T lever, the drain wheel ("persons in the cistern") and the shortcut door.
   - Ines in person should mention Pell after the soup note.
   - Remove the stray "charges are with Dario… your hands, your demons" line from the ending.
9. **Personal threads in the ending.** A fate line for Dario that reflects backing him and flirting with him (David's homoerotic thread deserves a payoff), plus reflection lines for the hidden meters David named: how much he flirted, which demons he used, his armor.
10. **Polish:**
    - The Compendium registers demons you fought.
    - A serpent portrait for Gonggong.
    - Camera voids.
    - Remove "Test rooms".
    - Status-bar refresh after paying and after chests.
    - Make Gonggong's 800-gold tribute not the last key, or follow it with a boss fight unless he was truly won over.
